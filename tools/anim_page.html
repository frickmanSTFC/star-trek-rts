Colony Pod Drop

What the player sees when they order a new colony on a Class M world. The drop ship lowers the pod on a tractor beam, then a construction beam circles the site twice to unfold the solar petals, drop the supply capsules and light the perimeter.

0.0 s

    In the game engine

    Each layer here (ship, pod, legs, petals, capsules, ramp) becomes a separate model or sprite part driven by one timeline: Unity Timeline, Godot AnimationPlayer or Unreal Sequencer. The beats on the right are the keyframes.

    Beams and effects

    The tractor cone and construction beam are shader effects in-engine (additive glow, scrolling rings) plus a particle system for the sparkles and dust. Here they are drawn live in SVG so the timing can be judged.

    Gameplay hooks

    The building becomes selectable at touchdown (5.0 s) and finishes at 12.6 s. The drop can be interrupted: shooting down the ship during the tractor phase loses the pod.
