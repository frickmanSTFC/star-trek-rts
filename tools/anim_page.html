Colony Pod Drop

What the player sees when they order a new colony on a Class M world. Twelve seconds from order to online. The pod materializes in the drop ship's tractor beam and is lowered to the surface, then a construction beam circles the site twice: the solar petals unfold, and the supply capsules, beacons and first colonists beam in.

0.0 s

    In the game engine

    Each layer here (ship, pod, legs, petals, capsules, ramp) becomes a separate model or sprite part driven by one timeline: Unity Timeline, Godot AnimationPlayer or Unreal Sequencer. The beats on the right are the keyframes.

    Beams and effects

    The tractor cone and construction beam are shader effects in-engine (additive glow, scrolling rings). The transporter shimmer is a dissolve shader on the model plus a rising-sparkle particle column. Here they are drawn live in SVG so the timing can be judged.

    Gameplay hooks

    The building becomes selectable at touchdown (4.6 s) and finishes at 12.0 s. The drop can be interrupted: shooting down the ship during the tractor phase loses the pod.
