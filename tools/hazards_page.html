Galactic Hazards

Everything in the Star Trek RTS that can hurt you besides other players: anomalies, trouble at your colonies, raiders, late-game crises and wild cards. Each entry says where it appears, what it does and how to answer it, and the last sections show how well each faction can field those answers. All effects are placeholders for balancing.
