Starship Designs

How the seven playable factions of the Star Trek RTS differ in the ships they fly. Every ship is a hull plus modules: the hull sets size, hull points, speed, crew and slot counts, and each faction's classes are templates on those hulls. All stats are placeholders for balancing.
