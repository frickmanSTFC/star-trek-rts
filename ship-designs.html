Starship Designs

How the seven playable factions of the Star Trek RTS differ in the ships they fly. Every ship is a hull plus modules: the hull sets size, hull points, speed, crew and slot counts, and each faction's classes are templates on those hulls. Each class also has a build cost, build time, crew cost and tech gate worked out from its hull and role, and the civilian fleet (freighters, colony transports, construction tenders, survey ships, tankers) is listed alongside the warships. All stats are placeholders for balancing.

Hull reference

Shared by every faction. Hull points are drawn to one scale; slot pips show how many modules of each kind fit (W weapon, D defense, E engine, U utility). Complement is the canon crew (lore); crew cost, base cost and build time are what a combat class on the hull takes, before its role scales them (see Build costs).

Section 3

Class roster

Every class on its hull, faction by faction. Pick a starting era to highlight what each faction can field in it: classes stay in service after the era they arrive in, and the Kelvin timeline only has its own ships.

Dashed cards are civilian and support classes (freighters, colony transports, construction tenders, survey ships, tankers); the rest are warships.

Section 6

Build costs

What each class takes to build, how long, how much of the crew resource it uses and the tech it needs first. The numbers come from one formula on hull size and role (data/ships/rules.json, written by tools/ship_costs.py), not picked by hand.

Section 7

Data checks

Every class has a cost, build time, crew cost and tech gate; costs use resources that exist; tech ids resolve, the faction can get the tech and its tier fits the era; roles are known; the numbers match the formula; each great power that researches has a civilian fleet with a construction ship. This list should be empty.
