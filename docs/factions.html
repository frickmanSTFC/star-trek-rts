Factions of the Galaxy

Every power in the Star Trek RTS. The seven great powers get a full comparison and a dossier that gathers what sets them apart on every other design page: buildings, fleets, research, craft, worlds, resources and hazards. Era powers, minor powers, pirates and crises follow. All values are placeholders for balancing.
