Click any recipe to open its full card; greyed-out recipes are ones you can't make right now. The 13 Machine Shop items

One illustration per item, in one style (3/4 view, light from the top left, kits as crates in their kit colour). These go on every recipe tile and on the full card, and can become the game's icons.

Applies to every option

  • No cut-off text, anywhere. Labels sit above values, long lines wrap, and anything that still can't fit gets a hover card, never an ellipsis in the middle of a word.
  • Can't make it right now? It's greyed out. Every recipe whose inputs you don't have is dimmed (3 of 13 can be made in this example). Clicking any recipe, greyed or not, opens its full card: what it needs against what you have, how long it takes, what it's for, and links to make the missing parts. Try it in any option.
  • Short means red with the reason. "Needs 2 Seal Kit · you have 0", not just "SHORT".
  • Same layout for the Vehicle Bay and Research Lab, which have the same problem with their order and tech lists.
  • Scales with the UI scale setting (85–125%) and is checked at 1280×720 and 1600×900.
