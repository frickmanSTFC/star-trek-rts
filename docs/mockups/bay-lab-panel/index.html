Vehicle Bay and Research Lab panels

The tabbed layout you picked for the Machine Shop, applied to the other two buildings with lists. Same rules: nothing cut off, anything you can't do right now is greyed out, and clicking any tile opens its full card. Vehicles use their real renders; each research tech shows the building or vehicle it improves. Names, costs, times and effects are the game's real data.

How these behave

  • Vehicle Bay. Tabs Overview · Queue · Vehicles. A vehicle you can't afford is greyed out with the reason (here the Mk III Explorer: needs 80 tritanium, you have 63). Click a tile for its card; Ctrl+click or a number key orders one, Shift orders five, right-click cancels one, as in the Machine Shop.
  • Research Lab. Tabs Overview · Tech tree · Done. Techs whose prerequisite isn't researched yet (here Automated Maintenance, which needs Preventive Maintenance) are greyed out with "needs …" and a line to the tech they wait on; finished techs show a green tick; the one being researched glows orange with its progress. Click any tech for its card; the card says what it changes in numbers and how long it takes at this lab's speed.
  • Situational techs (Artifact Analysis, Xenomedicine) are marked, since they only matter with ruins.
