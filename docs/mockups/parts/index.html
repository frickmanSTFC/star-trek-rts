Parts Economy

Ore becomes metal, metal becomes materials, the Machine Shop turns materials into parts, and parts become the spare-part kits that repairs use and the construction parts that new buildings need. Broken buildings now wait on that chain. Hover the diagram, run the shop, open the inventory and try each repair case. The era bar changes the recipes, wear and repair times everywhere on the page.

Queue+1 / +5 add to the end, × cancels and refunds the inputs of a started order. One production line at Mk I; Mk II would add a second.

Keep in stocka target per item. When stock + queued is below it, the shop queues one more (blue AUTO tag). A kit queued this way also queues the parts it is missing.

Shortagesan order without its inputs is skipped (red tag) and named in the warnings with the building that makes the missing input.

Powerthe shop draws 8 while working. In the Dominion War era, Replicate mode makes parts from power alone: 40% of the time, +30 power, which overloads this base.

EEras change the economy

The parts economy matters most in the early eras. By the Dominion War an industrial replicator can turn power into parts, so the limit becomes the power budget. Dawn of Warp is a proposed new first era; Enterprise-era wear and repair numbers are placeholders of mine.
