Part 1

Unlock badge

Behaviour

  • Where: top centre, under the top bar, above the map. In orbit it sits under the helm title. It never covers the build drawer, the event feed or the bottom panel.
  • How long: 4.5 s, or 6 s for gold. Hovering holds it.
  • Several at once: they stack, up to 3, newest at the bottom. More wait their turn.
  • Sound: a short chime per tier. It follows the game's effects volume.
  • Also logged: one line in the event feed ("Achievement: Refined Taste"), so a missed badge can still be seen.
  • Not shown: in test mode, with draft data, or while the F1 help or a large card is open. Those wait until it closes.

Part 2

Achievements screen

Opening it

  • The trophy chip in the top bar shows how many are earned. Click it, press F3, or click a badge while it's showing.
  • It also opens from the WORLD chooser panel, so it can be checked between games.
  • The game keeps running underneath, as with the build card. Esc closes it.

What it shows

  • Summary: earned of total, points (bronze 10, silver 25, gold 50), and the count for each tier.
  • Categories down the left, with earned / total for each.
  • Earned cards show the date, the world, and the game time it happened.
  • In progress cards show a bar ("62 of 100 hexes") for counts, shares and streaks.
  • Hidden ones read "Hidden achievement" until earned. The tier still shows, so players know one is there.
