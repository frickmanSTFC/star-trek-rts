Your officer, saves and comms

Who you play: not the captain but the outpost's second, heading one of four departments under the head of the outpost. The new-campaign flow gains an officer step (identity, looks, background, department), the main menu and pause menu gain load and save, and people talk to you on a view screen. How it would work

  • Where it sits. Campaign → faction → era → your officer (new, five steps) → pick a world → land. DEBUG skips it with a default officer.
  • Your role. You still give every order (it's an RTS). Your department is where you shine: its perks apply to your colony, the head of the outpost sends you missions about it, and the other three departments are run by officers who report to you on comms and handle their own routine work (the maintenance chief books repairs, security answers hazards) unless you take over.
  • Species by faction and era. Dawn of Warp is an Earth expedition, so Humans only. From the Enterprise era Vulcans, Andorians and Tellarites open; later eras add Trill, Betazoids and Bajorans. Other powers have their own lists (Borg get a designation, not a name).
  • Looks. The picture you pick is a starting face; sliders and swatches change the live bust (the crew figures' variant hash, so your walker on the map matches your portrait).
  • Saves. Autosave at landing, then every 5 minutes (3 rotating slots), plus quicksave (Ctrl+S) and named saves from the pause menu. The load screen groups saves by officer, newest first.
  • How a save is made (proposal). A full snapshot of the simulation state, checked against its state hash when loaded. The command log rides along so an older save can still be replayed if the format changes. Saves from builds whose data no longer matches are flagged before you load them.
  • Comms. A view screen with the speaker's bust, name, post and signal delay (the game already has signal delay). Messages from Earth in Dawn of Warp arrive recorded with the supply ship. Later, rival and native leaders hail you live.

Open questions

  1. Department perks. Real bonuses (as shown), or flavour only with missions as the difference?
  2. Species perks. Small bonuses (proposed), or looks only?
  3. Sex. Female and male only, or also a third option (and should pronouns be picked separately)?
  4. Traits. Pick 2 (Humans 3) as shown? Add an optional flaw that buys an extra trait?
  5. Can you lose your post? E.g. relieved after a disaster, then continue as another officer in the same colony?
  6. One officer per campaign, or carry the same officer from world to world (with their history and achievements)?
  7. Your peers: randomly generated, or can you pick or rename them?
  8. Head of the outpost: a fixed character per campaign, or generated? Should they ever be replaced or promote you?
  9. Save format: snapshot (recommended) or replay-only (smaller, but breaks whenever the sim changes)?
  10. Ironman option (one autosave, no reloading)?
  11. Voices: do comms get spoken lines later (a voice choice is on the Appearance step), or subtitles with a talk sound?
  12. Your officer on the map: a walker you can follow (it rides along on missions), or only a portrait?
