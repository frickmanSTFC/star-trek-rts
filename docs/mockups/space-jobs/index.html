Space Jobs

Wrecks, debris fields, asteroids, anomalies and lifepods placed between the bodies of the system. You find them with the scanner, fly out and work them. Any ship can take them as side jobs, and each space chain gets two new main missions built on them.

How it plays

Space sites

Each system rolls 4 to 7 sites at the start, placed off the orbits between bodies. More arrive during the match (about one every 6 minutes): a distress call, a comet passing through, a rogue asteroid, a ship that limps in.

  • Detected: a grey contact with a size. The long-range scan finds them like bodies.
  • Identified: fly within 120 map units and scan (20–45 s). Kind, hazards and a first look at what is there.
  • Worked: the ship is alongside or inside the site. Most work is time on site plus choices.
  • Spent: the site stays on the plot, greyed, until it drifts out after 5 minutes.

Side-job pool

Every ship gets side jobs, not only the colony. A site offers its job when it is identified (or when an event arrives); at most 2 jobs run at once, offers lapse after 90 s and come back once a slot is free.

  • Main chains go on as now. Each space chain also gets two new missions built on sites (below).
  • Failing a job still costs only its reward, but the hull damage and crew lost on the way stay.
  • Which divisions you took in the fit-out decide how good a ship is at each kind of job.

Hull damage

The flagship gets hull integrity, 0–100, and four systems that can be knocked out: sensors, engines, tractor, life support.

  • 75–100 scuffed, no effect. 50–75 flights +10%. 25–50 flights +25%, scan reach −20%. Below 25 no hazardous jobs, flights +60%.
  • At 0 crippled: the ship drifts where it is, every active job fails, field repairs bring it back to 20% over 3 minutes. No game over.
  • Repairs: Engineering crew patch it up to 60% for free (speed from the Engineering share); hull patch kits from the hold add 15% each; a dock at the colony or an allied station restores 100%.
  • When combat arrives it uses this same bar.

Crew loss

Crew are counted by division, as the fit-out set them. Away teams and some hazards roll per person.

  • Injured: out for 3 minutes, then back. Lost: gone for the match.
  • A division's share sets its effects (0.4% a point, as in the fit-out), so a lost pilot makes scans a little slower.
  • Below a division's 10% floor its effects stop and the ship card warns. A ship needs half its crew to fly at full speed.
  • Defense on the team lowers the loss chance; a Science medic turns half the losses into injuries; medical stores shorten injuries.
  • The Armstrong (6 crew) can send a team of 2 at most, so its jobs lean to towing and runs, not boarding.

One job in full: The Quiet Hulk

Side job · any ship with 3 or more crew · offered by Science. “Long-range contact near Kessa IV: a hull with no power and no transponder. Nobody has filed a flight plan for it. Go and look.”

1
Identify. Fly within range and scan it (30 s × the sweep lever). Reveals size, design (unknown), power (one trickle reading), life (a faint reading, 50% real).
2
Come alongside. 20 s next to it (alongside lever). One debris roll: 40% for −2 to −6 hull.
3
Away team. Three steps, 60 s each, the game never pauses; a step that runs out takes its first choice.
  1. Who goes: 2–6 crew from any division.
  2. How in: their airlock (slow, rigged 20%) · cut the hull (fast, decompression 12%) · space-walk to the bridge (Exploration, finds +15).
  3. What to bring back: the data core (research, opens a follow-up) · the power cell (a part, takes 1 container) · the life reading (if real: a survivor who joins the crew; if not, nothing).
4
Leave. The team is back aboard after 20 s. The job completes; leaving orbit earlier fails it.

What can happen (rolled once, after step 3)

60%Clean. Research 120 and the chosen item.
20%Power surge. Hull −12, one team member injured, item still recovered.
12%Defences wake. Each team member rolls loss at 8% (−4 per Defense member, half become injuries with a medic). Item recovered on a coin flip.
8%Something came back with them. The team is in quarantine 3 min (counts as injured); Science stores shorten it.

The step 2 choice shifts these odds (screen 2 shows it live). The data core opens “Where They Came From”: a second site, far out, with the hulk's home beacon.

In the data

The same missions format as now (data/missions/side_jobs.json): new conditions site (identified / alongside / boarded / swept / mined), hull, crew, hold; new actions place_space_site, away_team (the event), damage, lose_crew, give_crew. Sites live in the star system state and are hashed with it.

New main missions per chain

Tereshkova · The Long Survey

  • Wreck at the Edge: a debris field past the last orbit; sweep it and recover the black box.
  • Hollow Rock: survey the belt, find the one asteroid with a cavity, send a team in.

Armstrong · Priority Run

  • Twelve Lifepods: a liner broke up; pick up pods against their air timers. Small hold, so several trips to the nearest station.
  • Tow Line: drag a disabled freighter to a body before its reactor fails.

Bastion · Watch Station

  • Rogue Rock: an asteroid on course for an inhabited moon; reach it and push it off course in time.
  • The Smuggler's Belt: a ship hiding in the belt; timed scans to flush it out, then inspect.

Meridian · Envoy

  • The Probe That Talks: decode an alien probe's signal; a good answer makes the later first contact easier.
  • Their Lost Ship: a native ship adrift; return it (towed or crewed) for trust, or study it and risk an incident.

Fit-out ties

Division levers (new)

  • Exploration: site scan time, debris-field search, lifepod pick-up.
  • Engineering: field repair speed, mining rate, tow speed, push strength.
  • Science: anomaly research, decoding, quarantine length.
  • Defense: away-team loss chance, trap detection.

New space cargo

  • Hull patch kit (Engineering, 1 container): +15% hull or fixes a system at once.
  • Medical stores (Science, space version): injuries 1 min shorter each, max 3.
  • Cutting gear (Engineering, 1 container): mining +25%.
  • Ore, salvage and survivors take hold space too, so a full hold is a real choice.

Open questions

  1. Mined ore: where does it go on a ship that never lands?

    Suggested: sell it to trade freighters and stations for latinum (a new score for space ships), or hand it in for jobs that ask for it. Upgrading the ship with it is bigger work.

  2. Do the Horizon and Cochrane get space jobs too?

    Suggested: yes, but their flagship stays near the colony body, so only sites within one flight; leaving orbit blocks drop ships and landings as today.

  3. Can lost crew be replaced?

    Suggested: survivors from rescues can join (by division), and a station visit hires 2 a time. Otherwise losses stay for the match.

  4. Crippled at 0 hull: is drifting with all jobs failed enough, or should it end the scenario?

    Suggested: drift and recover. No game over until combat exists.

  5. Four ship systems (sensors, engines, tractor, life support), or only the hull bar?

    Systems make anomalies and traps more varied; the hull bar alone is simpler to read.

  6. Towing: is it worth it?

    Flights 1.6× slower while towing, wrecks and derelicts can be brought to a body for more reward. Adds the Tow Line mission.

  7. Which first?

    Suggested order: sites + scanning + hull damage + the derelict and debris jobs (with the away team), then asteroids and mining, then events (rogue asteroid, comet, lifepods), then the chain missions.
