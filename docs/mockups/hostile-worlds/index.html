Hostile Worlds

What it takes to land on a world that is not Class M: three example surfaces side by side, the new things on the map (lava that advances and cools, eruptions, CO₂ pockets, dust storms, radiation), and the HUD for suits, domes, shields and life support. It builds on the Gravity & Hostile Worlds design doc: its six habitability tiers, one unique hook per class, and its phasing.

1 · The examples

Three worlds, three kinds of hostile

Each world keeps the shared rules (gravity curves, protection tier) and adds one hook of its own. Veyra and Tarsis A are bodies on the Kalandra duel map. The volcanic world is new: no class in the data has lava, so this page proposes Class F (geometallic), a tidally heated moon of Hesper and Ilium, like Io round Jupiter.

The brief's example line read "Domes · CO₂ 4.5 %, O₂ 13.5 %, −15 °C". With the doc's thresholds those numbers come out as Masks (O₂ 12–19.5 % and gases under 20× the limit), so Veyra stays Masks here and Domes is shown on Cinder and Tarsis A. CO₂ is heavier than air, so on calm nights it pools in Veyra's basins above 20× the limit: those hexes need suits.

2 · Surface look

Real renders at the game camera

Each world is a patch of the game's own hex terrain (pointy-top hexes 25 units wide, odd rows shifted) built and rendered in Blender by blender/build_hostile_worlds.py, with the game's glbs for scale: outpost_l1, refinery, the Mk II and Mk III rovers, the mining crawler and the construction rover. Three cameras: the strategy pitch at zoom 290, the eased pitch at zoom 50 (PlanetDriver's camera formula), and the outpost's video feed (fov 52).

3 · New surface mechanics

Hazards on the map, minimap and tooltip

The overlays are drawn over the strategy render using the real projected hex corners. Hover a hex for its tooltip, step the clock to see the lava advance and cool, and switch worlds. Lava hexes become impassable; buildings next to a flow take heat damage and get a warning ring before it arrives.

What the player reads

Lava (Cinder)A sim system, not scenery: each lava tile has an age; the front advances one hex every 60–120 s along the steepest descent (map-seeded, no sim.Rng so replays match), then the oldest tiles cool to crust (passable at ×0.3 speed, tyre wear ×3). Lava tiles are impassable in FindPath; a structure on or next to one takes heat wear.
EruptionsScheduled per vent from the seed, warned 40 s ahead: a red ring on the vent's neighbours, a helm finding, an event line. Bombs land in the ring (damage 10–30, like a rover hazard hit).
Dust storms, CO₂ (Veyra)Already in the climate sim (dust_storm spells drift −2 tiles/min). New: a CO₂ pocket is a tile state computed from night + low ground + calm wind, read by Telemetry as a toxic patch, so the existing toxic tooltip and rover damage apply.
Radiation, micrometeorites (Tarsis A)Micrometeorites become a weather-like spell of struck hexes (the doc's hazard row, damage 6–18). A stellar flare raises radiation everywhere for 45 s; only crews in a shelter or dome are safe. The terminator sweeps the map in the 24-minute locked day.
4 · HUD and UI

Scanning, surviving and building inside the line

The game's two frames: the helm screen in orbit (HelmScreen.cs colours, slim elbows) and the surface HUD (PlanetHud.cs). Everything here is a mockup drawn in HTML.

Landing kitOn a Domes world the three drop ships bring the outpost's radius-1 dome (the doc's rule), so the opening still works without research.

Top bar: the life-support chip

The chip sits beside the climate chip, only on worlds above Open. Three gauges: air reserve, heat, dome integrity; the power for life support is part of the base's budget. Hover opens the per-base breakdown (shown open here). On Masks worlds it shrinks to a mask icon with the crew-time penalty.

If lava is what the owner wants to see first

Lava cannot come before the domes kit: Cinder has no O₂, so it needs domes, life support and sealing first. Two ways to get there sooner: (a) build phase 2 on Cinder instead of Tarsis A (domes kit + lava together, ~8 sessions before it plays, one big step), or (b) a look-only lava pass after phase 1: the terrain set, lava shader and vents on a Cinder map you can land on with the Test panel, no lava rules yet (~2 sessions), then the rules after phase 2. The page recommends (b): it shows the world early without skipping the parts it depends on.

7 · Questions for the owner

One word back per line is enough

  1. Volcanic world as a new Class F (geometallic) moon of Hesper/Ilium? Or a volcanic variant of an existing class (H with lava fields, K with old volcanoes)? Recommended: Class F, two twin bodies so the duel map stays symmetric.
  2. Lava as a sim system (tiles advance, cool, block paths, damage buildings), or scenery plus scripted eruption events? Recommended: a sim system; it is the world's whole point.
  3. Domes as drawn: a glass dome over the core's own hex plus a containment field reaching 1 (outpost) or 2 tiles (Pressure Dome)? Or one giant glass dome over the whole radius? Recommended: as drawn; hexes are 25 units and buildings ~10, so a glass dome of 2 tiles would be 125 units wide.
  4. First hostile world: Veyra (the doc's pick) or straight to the domes kit? And is the look-only lava pass after Veyra wanted?
  5. Veyra's tier: the doc's thresholds give Masks, not Domes. Keep Masks (with suits only in night CO₂ pockets), or tighten the thresholds so Veyra needs domes?
  6. Lava Barrier: a passive berm that wears while holding lava (drawn), or a powered heat-shield field (costs power, fails on brown-outs)? Or both, as Mk I and Mk II?
  7. Veyra's polar caps: the data's poles reach about −62 °C, too warm for CO₂ ice at Veyra's pressure. Raise polar_drop for CO₂ frost, or call the caps water ice?
  8. EVA teams: a selectable unit with an air timer (drawn), or crews that only walk out for jobs like today's repair crews?
  9. Ash fall: a weather kind that cuts solar and visibility and adds wear (like dust), or visual only?
  10. Life-support failure: lose crew (the doc's rule), or slow down first and only lose crew at zero air, as in the failure order drawn?
