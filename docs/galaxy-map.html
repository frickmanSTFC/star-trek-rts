Galaxy Map

Known space for the Star Trek RTS: every faction's territory, the warp lanes between systems, and how long each trip takes, both in the lore (by warp factor) and in a match (by the propulsion tech you've researched). Matches use sector maps of 6 to 24 systems cut from this master map. Positions are approximate where the shows give no figures.

Section 3

Warp scale

Voyager's famous "75 years home" from the Delta Quadrant assumes cruising speed, stops for fuel and repairs, and detours; flying flat out at warp 9.975 the straight line would take under nine.

Section 4

Drives in a match

What each propulsion tech does to travel on the map. Game speed is in light-years per minute of play.

Section 5

Distances between capitals

Light-years along the shortest lane route (straight-line distance below), and minutes of play at Warp 8. Routes use the stable Bajoran wormhole but not the unstable Barzan one.

Section 6

Territories

Every faction with ground on the map: its capital, quadrant, distance from Earth and the eras it appears in.
