Achievements

30 achievements8 categories750 points30 cosmetics

Every achievement of the game prototype, by category, as data/achievements/ defines it. Each one earns a cosmetic (a rover name, a hull number, a paint, a finish, a drop ship name, an exocomp beam colour or a dome tint), never anything that changes play. The simulation checks the rules about once a second of game time; nothing counts in test mode, with draft data or in the balance runner. Hidden ones show as question marks in the game until earned. All thresholds are fixed in the data.

Trigger kinds

How a rule is written (see data/achievements/notes.json for every milestone, event, measure and condition name).

KindFieldsNames
milestonemilestone, before_seconds, withoutA one-time moment: uplink (the outpost's uplink comes online); landed (the colony lands); first_metal (an Ore Refinery works); first_parasteel (the first parasteel is in stock); second_base (two finished colony cores); research_all (every surface tech is known); built:<building>; unit:<unit>. 5 used
builtbuilding, countThat many finished buildings of one id at once. 3 used
countmeasure, at_least | allbodies_charted (bodies charted); system_satellites (satellites out in the system); units (vehicles on the ground); techs_known (surface techs known). 4 used
sharemeasure, at_least (0–1)surveyed: the share of the map's tiles the player has surveyed. 1 used
unit_countercounter, at_least, unitstiles_driven: hex edges one vehicle of those units has crossed. 1 used
eventevent + its fieldsorbit_farthest; charted_class [class]; deposit_found [amount]; founded; deposit_reopened; tyre_changed_field; limped_home; crawler_explosion; domed_crewed_working; life_support_saved; order_relayed [delay_seconds]; sos_answered [within_seconds]. 12 used
streakcondition, seconds, from_startno_power_short (no base short of power); flagship_home (the flagship in orbit of the colony's body); from_start = must hold from the first check. 2 used
setmeasure, containsweather_seen: weather kinds over the player's vehicles and cores. 1 used
view_countmeasure, at_least | allships_inspected: counted by the game's view (the sim never unlocks it). 1 used
all_of / any_ofrulesEvery / any of the rules (one level, no view_count inside). 0 used
