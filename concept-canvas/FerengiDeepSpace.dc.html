Deep Space line · Ferengi Alliance · 4 levels

Trading Post to Grand Exchange

Every Ferengi station is a shop. Bulbous copper domes with gold latinum trim and lime-green signs pull in traffic, and each level turns more passing ships into profit. Other factions pay to dock here.

LV 1

Trading Post

One copper dome with a lit sign and two cargo pods. It claims the system and charges a small latinum fee on every freighter, of any faction, that passes through.

Bazaar Station

The dome grows a ring of rented shop pods. Any player's freighter can trade here, and the Ferengi skim a cut of every deal. It also sells repairs to anyone who pays.

Marauder Station

The Ferengi starbase copies the D'Kora's horseshoe on a huge scale. It hires mercenaries: pay latinum and pirate or minor-faction ships appear at its docks.

Grand Exchange
