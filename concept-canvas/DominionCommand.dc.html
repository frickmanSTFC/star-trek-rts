Vorta Field Post to Founders' Sanctuary

Vorta run Dominion worlds for the Founders, with Jem'Hadar guarding them. Buildings are ridged shells with beetle-wing canopies. At the top of the line the architecture gives way to the Founders themselves: a living golden sea.

LV 1

Vorta Field Post

A ridged dome run by a Vorta field supervisor, with Jem'Hadar guard spires and a White cache. If the supervisor is killed, a replacement clone arrives after a delay.

Founders' Sanctuary

The capital is not a building: it is the Great Link, a sea of joined Changelings, with one Vorta sanctuary on the crags. It trains Changeling infiltrators and boosts the loyalty of every Vorta and Jem'Hadar in the Dominion.
