Habitation line · Ferengi Alliance · 3 levels

Rental Habitat to Resort Spire

On a Ferengi world nobody owns their home; they rent it. Every habitation level produces latinum as well as population, and the top level attracts paying visitors from other factions.

LV 1

Rental Habitat

Copper pods stacked two high, each with its rate posted on the door. Tenants pay rent every cycle. Raise the rent for more latinum, at the cost of morale.

Population cap+1 Rent+1 Latinum (adjustable) Cost60 Dur
LV 2

Market Quarter

Shops at street level and rental pods above. The tenants work the shops below, so the district pays twice: rent plus a cut of every sale. It also sells luxury goods that raise morale.

Population cap+3 IncomeRent + 10% of sales Cost150 Dur · 60 Latinum
MAX

Resort Spire

A tower of holosuite balconies next to a giant dabo wheel. Visitors from any faction you trade with spend latinum here, and their crews come home with lower morale and lighter wallets.

Population cap+6 in 1 slot Tourism+3 Latinum / trade partner Cost300 Par · 150 Latinum
