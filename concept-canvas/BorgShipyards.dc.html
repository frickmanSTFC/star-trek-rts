Assembly Matrix to Cube Matrix

Borg yards are lattices that a hull grows inside. Each level is shaped like the ship it builds: a probe frame, then a spherical cage, then a cube. Hulls are partly made from the wrecks of ships the Collective has destroyed.

Assembly Matrix

A flat lattice that grows Borg probes. It tractors in wreckage from the system (bottom right) and returns part of the cost of every hull it builds as salvage.

Sphere Matrix

The lattice closes into a cage that grows Borg spheres, half by half. Spheres can launch from inside a cube later. Any foreign tech the Collective has assimilated can be built into them.

Cube Matrix
