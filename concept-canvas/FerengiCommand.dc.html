Command line · Ferengi Alliance · 3 levels

Trade Office to Tower of Commerce

On Ferenginar it always rains. Ferengi government is a business, so the command line is a chain of offices that sells licenses, collects fees and unlocks Rules of Acquisition as the colony's profit grows.

LV 1

Trade Office

A copper dome with a lit sign and a gold door. It founds the colony and starts earning latinum right away, but has no garrison. Defense is something you buy.

Supply range1 Income+2 Latinum Cost90 Dur · 30 Latinum
LV 2

Commerce Authority Office

A branch of the Ferengi Commerce Authority: stacked domes with flared fins. It sells trade licenses to other players and unlocks the first Rule of Acquisition perk.

Supply range2 Unlocks1 Rule perk · licenses Cost260 Dur · 120 Latinum
MAX · FERENGI ONLY

Tower of Commerce

The Grand Nagus's seat: stacked copper tiers under a gold crown shaped like spread ears. It unlocks every Rule of Acquisition perk and lets you bribe AI factions and rival governors.

Supply range3 UnlocksAll Rules · bribery Cost400 Par · 500 Latinum
