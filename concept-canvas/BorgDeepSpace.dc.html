Deep Space line · Borg Collective · 4 levels

Probe Node to Transwarp Hub

Borg structures don't grow into new shapes; they grow by adding more. Each level bolts more black cubes and tubing onto the last, lit in sickly green. Their real purpose is linking: every node shares what the Collective has learned.

Probe Node

One small cube that links the system to the Collective. It sees every ship in the system and records the weapons they use against it.

Vinculum Station

The cubes fuse into one big block around a vinculum, the core that links every drone nearby. Borg ships in range regenerate fast. Destroy the vinculum and every drone in range is cut off from the Collective and stops for a while.
