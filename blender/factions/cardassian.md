# Cardassian ground kit (Blender mockups)

The Cardassian kit is the rival's counterpart to the Federation rover, crawler, lander and Colony Hub. It is built for occupation, not exploration: heavy, armoured and a little menacing. Hulls are tan (#b0905e) over ochre (#8a6a44), brown (#5e4a32) and brown-grey (#8a8070) running gear. Every model is made of **layered ribbed segments**: carapace plates that step down the roof, ribbed skirts, ribbed shells and walls. A **forward-swept fan or crescent** shows up on each one: the rover's ram prow and hooded visor, the crawler's dozer blade, the drop ship's whole planform, the sensor vanes, the portal hoods, the monument. Windows are narrow slits glowing amber behind dark glass. Lights are amber-orange, with forked antennas and spires tipped in amber. The player colour ("Owner") shows as one dark-red stripe, rib or banner. These are original designs in the faction's general style, built from this project's own concept boards (`concept-canvas/Cardassian*.dc.html`). None of them copies a ship, vehicle or set from the shows.

- **Survey rover** (`--model rover`, TARVEK 31): a 5.0 × 2.6 × 2.3 six-wheeler (antenna to 3.3), close to the Federation Mk I (4.4 × 2.5 × 2.2) but heavier. Its wheels sit under ribbed brown-grey skirts. Up front are a crescent ram prow with forward horns and cheek fins that hood a slit visor. Five carapace plates step down the roof. On top are a spinning fan-vane sensor head (`Dish`), a forked antenna and a `Beacon`. Decal empties `NameL/R`, `NameRear` and `NumberL/R`.
- **Mining crawler** (`--model miner`, `--pose deploy|travel`, DRAVAN 09): about 5.3 × 2.7 × 3.1 in travel pose, on two armoured tracks. It has a crescent dozer blade, an offset hooded cab, a power unit with stacks and a flared, ribbed ore hopper (`Fill`). The drill boom (`Boom` > `Drill` > `String` > `Bit`, `Dust`) folds back over the hopper for travel and swings forward to drill. Four stabiliser legs (`Leg0-3`, with `Leg{k}Ext` telescoping and `Leg{k}Foot` counter-rotated) splay out like a spider when deployed.
- **Drop ship** (`--model dropship`, `--cargo miner|rover|none`, VEKTAL 4): 12.8 long and 12.4 wide. A flat, ribbed lifting body sits between forward-swept fan wings that droop to wingtip landing legs, giving an arrowhead or fan shape from the game camera. It has two rear engines (`EngineL/R`) and ventral lift glows. Instead of a ramp it carries one vehicle in a belly cargo clamp (`CargoClamp`, arms `Clamp0-3`); the preview shows the crawler clamped in its travel pose. Landing legs are `Leg0-3`.
- **Garrison Post L1** (`--model outpost --level 1`): just landed. A ribbed bunker pod (10 × 7.4 × 4.9) stands on fan buttresses inside a scorch ring. It has an oval amber portal (`Door`) under a crescent hood with cheek fins, a forked antenna, a sensor head, floodlight pylons, supply crates and a small pad, all inside one hex (the hex outline `HexGuide` is preview only). The rover is parked for scale.
- **Prefecture L5** (`--level 5`): sits on a hex platform inside one hex. A sloped two-tier ribbed seat has a barrel-vault upper tier, an oval portico under a crescent hood, two Owner banners and twin forked spires. Around it are a state broadcast `Screen` with an abstract emblem, a guard tower with a floodlight and `Beacon`, ore silos with a conveyor, a pad and wall segments.
- **Central Command L10** (`--level 10`): the capital, spanning the centre hex and spilling into its neighbours. A 52-tall ribbed HQ tower has flared shoulders, an amber seam and a forked crown. Colonnade wings carry banners and broadcast screens, and two flanking spires stand beside them. A fan monument faces a causeway, with four habitation towers behind. A monorail, silos and pads complete the base, with a parked drop ship and the rover for scale.

Renders: `blender/previews/factions/cardassian/` (`cardassian-<model>[-l<level>][-travel]-front34.png` / `-game.png`, the Federation Mk I under the same cameras as `federation-mk1-*.png`, and the labelled grid `cardassian-sheet.png`).
