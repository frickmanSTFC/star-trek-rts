# Romulan ground kit (mockups)

Built by `blender/factions/build_romulan.py` (see its docstring for flags); renders in `blender/previews/factions/romulan/`, overview in `romulan-sheet.png` (the Federation Mk I Scout is in the first cell for scale; the rover, miner and Fed rover share one camera).

The Romulan kit is meant to read as birds of prey even as a tiny icon. Deep green hulls sit over dark grey bellies, with pale jade waist lines and trailing bands. Every machine has crescent "wings" that mantle down over it like a hawk over its prey: on the rover they land on hover pods, on the drop ship they become landing legs, and on the buildings they are roofs and arches. The wings have feather-row panelling: chevron seams over the backs, staggered feather cuts on the top skins and loose feather blades fanning back from the trailing edges. The Owner colour runs along each leading edge (gold in the previews). Glows are cyan-green. Vehicles hover or stalk on talons instead of rolling on wheels, which suits the faction's secretive, cloaked style. All shapes are original designs in that general style and do not copy any existing ship or set.

- **Survey skimmer** (`--model rover`, 4.65 × 2.5 × 2.1 including the trailing feathers): hooked-beak body with a jade canopy. Two raised-shoulder wings droop onto claw-nosed hover pods that have glowing emitters underneath. Feather blades and a tail fan trail behind, the crest sensor head ("Dish") sits on the back and a green "Beacon" on the rump. Name and number are on the pods' flat flanks (NameL/R, NumberL/R with `--blank`).
- **Mining walker** (`--model miner`, body 4.9 long, 5.8 with the boom out × 2.75 × 3.15; `--pose deployed|stowed`): carapace with folded feathered wing armour, walking on four reverse-knee legs (Leg0–3) with three-toed talons. A drill boom (Boom, Drill, String, Bit, Dust) stoops from the beak like a striking raptor. The ore hopper has a glowing "Fill" window, and the name and number are on its flanks.
- **Talon drop ship** (`--model dropship`, 13.55 long, 13.1 span, 6.7 tall): crescent wings arch out and down until their tips stand on the ground as landing legs, with a tail strut as the third foot. A four-talon cargo clamp under the belly holds one vehicle (shown with the skimmer). It also has a beaked canopy, twin drive glows, a feather tail fan, a Dish and a Beacon.
- **Colony core L1 – Colonial Garrison** (`--model outpost --level 1`): just landed. A low hall stands on four splayed landing legs under a crescent wing roof whose feathered tips touch down. It has a ramp from the glowing door, a scorched landing ring, a feather-blade beacon spire, the Dish on the ridge and three drop pods of stores. It fits well inside one hex.
- **Colony core L5 – Proconsulate** (`--level 5`): a stone hex terrace. The hall's roof splits into two upswept crescent wings over a reflecting pool. There are owner banners on masts, three crescent-roofed villas, a landing pad and a taller beacon spire. It fits inside one hex, and from the game camera it reads as a bird with spread wings.
- **Colony core L10 – Imperial capital** (`--level 10`, about 2.5 hexes across, shown on a 7-hex outline): three terraces and a 54-unit feather-blade spire on a senate drum. Two great crescent arches (folded wings) frame the spire, joined to it by skybridges. There is a long reflecting pool and canal, a ring of seven crescent-roofed halls, and two secondary spires, one with the Dish and one with a beacon.
