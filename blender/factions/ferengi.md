# Ferengi ground kit (mockups)

Built by `blender/factions/build_ferengi.py`; renders in `blender/previews/factions/ferengi/` (`ferengi-sheet.png` puts every model next to the Federation Mk I Scout at the same camera).

**Look.** Everything is a shop. Hulls are bulbous copper superellipsoids (`FerCopper`, darker `FerCopperDark`) on burnt-umber chassis (`FerUmber`), with polished gold trim bands, ribs and hubs (`FerGold`). The recurring shape is the crescent or horseshoe: wheel fenders, sensor "ears", door arches, the drop ship's deck, the L5 fins and the capital's crown are all the same tapered-crescent primitive. Lights are deliberately showy: warm marquee bulbs (`MarqueeBulb`) run round waists, sign frames and plinths, windows glow amber (`WindowLit`, `FerGlass`), and the signs use lime lettering on black boards (`SignLime` / `SignBack`), like the lime signs on the concept boards. Everything carries a price: roof billboards ("ASSAYS 2 STRIPS"), ore tickers ("ORE 12 STRIPS / T"), cream swing tags ("FOR LEASE", "MAKE OFFER"), rent posted on every habitat door, commodity price tickers round the domes and pallets of gold-pressed latinum. The player colour (`Owner`, teal in the previews) is a waist stripe, guard stripes, pod bands and plinth bands. All of it is an original design in the general Ferengi style (copper, gold, ears, profit), not a copy of any ship, vehicle or set.

- **Prospector survey rover** (`--model rover`, 4.2 × 2.6 × 2.7 to the sensor tips; body 2.2 tall, like the Federation Mk I whose 4.4 "tall" on the sheet is its whip antenna): copper egg on four gold-hubbed wheels under crescent fenders, amber bubble canopy with a gold spine, a two-sided roof billboard, a crescent sensor with a lime eye (`Dish`, spins), `Beacon`, `TailLamp`s and a "FOR LEASE" tag. Name/number sit on raised nameplates (`NameL/NameR/NumberL/NumberR` empties).
- **Concession digger** (`--model miner`, `--pose stowed|deployed`, 5.4 × 2.9 × 3.0 stowed): twin tracks with gold road wheels, bulbous cab, open ore hopper (`Fill`) with lit tickers, a boom (`Boom`) that lies back over the cab with the drill (`Drill`, `String`, `Bit`) hanging in the gap in front of the hopper and swings 150° forward to drill ahead of the tracks; stabiliser legs `Leg0`–`Leg3` fold along the tracks and splay down to plant.
- **Freight yoke drop ship** (`--model dropship`, 8.6 × 9.0 × 5.3): a crescent deck (horns aft) on two bulbous engine pods with rear thrusters and lift rings, a bulbous bow bridge with lit windows and a "FREIGHT - NO REFUNDS" sign, landing gear `Gear0`–`Gear3`, and a yoke beam across the open middle whose four clamps (`Clamp0`–`Clamp3`) grip one vehicle by its chassis (shown carrying the rover).
- **L1 Trade Office, just landed** (`--model outpost --level 1`, 12.4 × 13.6 × 7.6, fits the hex): copper dome with gold ribs on an octagonal landing plinth, landing legs still out, scorch ring, gold door under a crescent arch with the lit "TRADE OFFICE" sign, a vertical "OPEN" blade sign on a mast with the `Beacon`, crescent sensor (`Dish`), two cargo pods for sale and a latinum pallet.
- **L5 Commerce Authority** (`--level 5`, 21.8 × 20.8 × 9.3, fits the hex): stacked domes (main, ticker ring, gold cap) with four flared crescent fins, entrance and sign, rental habitat stacks with their rents posted, a ticker tower ("BUY LOW / SELL HIGH / ALL SALES FINAL"), landing pad, cargo pods and a licence rate board.
- **L10 Tower of Commerce, capital** (`--level 10`, 32.5 × 31.2 × 29, bigger than one hex on purpose): five stacked bulbous copper tiers on a two-step podium, lime ticker rings and commodity boards, a gold crown of two outward ear-lobes holding a latinum orb on a spire, grand entrance, a clear vault of latinum bars, a prize-wheel sign, habitat stacks, two landing pads and two ticker towers.
