# Dominion ground kit (mockups)

Built by `blender/factions/build_dominion.py` (see its docstring for flags); renders in `blender/previews/factions/dominion/`, contact sheet `dominion-sheet.png`.

**Look.** Everything the Dominion puts on the ground looks grown rather than built, like a beetle. Each vehicle and building is a dark gunmetal body with deep violet wing-case (elytra) shells on top. The shells are split along the spine by a lavender glowing seam, have raised ridges along their length and dark grooves across them, and carry a gold "Owner" stripe on their lower edges. Underneath are dark ribbed bands, so the belly always reads as segmented. The front is a bug-eye canopy of dark violet glass over violet cheek plates, with blue-white lamp lenses. Lavender marks Dominion tech: repulsors, seams, sensor eyes and spire rings. Blue-white marks Ketracel-White: canisters, doors and clone pods. A glowing White canister is fitted to every vehicle and building, a reminder of the supply the Jem'Hadar can't live without. The Jem'Hadar side is utilitarian: armoured insect legs, clamps and guard spires. The Vorta side is polished: glossy coated shells, soft glows, the tall hall with its tower. All designs are original. They borrow only the general faction cues (violet ridged carapace, blue-white light, White canisters, guard spires) and copy no specific ship, vehicle or set. Conventions kept for the game: +Y front, `Owner` material, `Dish`, `Beacon`, `TailLamp*`, marker empties `NameL/NameR/NumberL/NumberR` (text shown unless `--blank`).

- **Survey rover** (`--model rover`, "VEKTA 07", 4.4 × 2.4 × 2.3): a hover skimmer instead of a wheeled truck. Its beetle shell rides 0.45 above the ground on four gunmetal repulsor pods with lavender emitters (point lights wash the sand under it). It has a neck collar and a bug-eye canopy. A spinning sensor head (`Dish`) with two curled prongs and a belly scanner lens map the deposits. A White canister sits in a cradle on the back, with `Beacon` on the tail.
- **Mining crawler** (`--model miner [--pose deployed|stowed]`, "DURAK 12", 5.3 × 3.1 × 3.3 stowed, 7.2 long with the boom unfolded): a six-legged walker (`Leg0`–`Leg5` empties at the hips) with armoured femurs and greaves. The rear wing cases part over an ore hopper (`Fill`). A mantis-like drill boom (`Boom` → elbow `Drill` → auger `String` → `Bit`) folds over the head for travel. When deployed it reaches about 4 units ahead and drives the auger into the deposit, with a spoil ring and a lavender glow where it bites.
- **Drop ship** (`--model dropship [--cargo rover|none]`, "ISHAN 21", 13.7 long incl. mandibles × 9.0 over the landing feet × 6.7): a beetle hull with a pronotum shield, swept antennae and forward mandibles. It carries one vehicle underneath in four jointed cargo clamps (`Clamp0`–`3`), like an insect holding its egg. It stands on four insect landing legs (`LandingLeg0`–`3`) and has two rear thruster nacelles and ventral repulsors. Three White canisters hang under the tail. The 3.45 belly clearance would also fit the 3.3-tall stowed crawler, but only the rover cargo pose is modelled.
- **Colony core L1** (`--model outpost --level 1`, Vorta Field Post, just landed, ~14 across inside the 25 hex): a ridged violet dome on a landing skirt with splayed feet, still-glowing thruster vents and a scorch ring. It has an arched blue-white door with a ramp, a crown with the `Beacon` and two curling prongs, two Jem'Hadar guard spires, a six-canister White cache and two cargo capsules.
- **Colony core L5** (`--model outpost --level 5`, Vorta Administration, 22.8 × 24.6, fits one hex): a ribbed hall under two sweeping wing-case canopies on jointed legs, a glowing seam on the crown spine, a window band and a 19-unit tower with the `Beacon`. Around it: a birthing chamber with four glowing clone pods, a White store piped to the hall, a landing pad with a parked rover and four guard spires.
- **Colony core L10** (`--model outpost --level 10`, Capital, ~76 × 73 × 44, spans the centre hex + 6 neighbours): the hall at double size with a 44-unit tower and two side towers. West: three cloning towers lined with pod columns and two birthing chambers. East: a six-canister White store. South: a basin of the golden Great Link among crags, with a small Vorta sanctuary on a rock pillar. Southeast: a landing field with two drop ships. A ring of ten guard spires surrounds it all.
- **Federation reference** (`--model fed`): `build_vehicles.py` Mk I Scout (tier 1, plain materials) under the same sky and cameras; `--model lineup` puts it beside the rover, the stowed crawler and the drop ship in one orthographic shot.
