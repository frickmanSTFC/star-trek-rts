Colony Pod Drop

What the player sees when they order a new colony on a Class M world. Twenty seconds from order to online. A compact pod materializes in the drop ship's tractor beam and is lowered to the surface. On the ground it unfurls: legs lift it, the saucer expands to full size, the command capsule and comm mast extend and the solar petals open. A construction beam then circles the site to beam in the supply capsules and beacons, and the first colonists beam down.

0.0 s

    In the game engine

    Each layer here (ship, saucer, command capsule, legs, mast, petals, capsules, ramp) becomes a separate model part, scaled and extended by bones or blend shapes, driven by one timeline: Unity Timeline, Godot AnimationPlayer or Unreal Sequencer. The beats on the right are the keyframes.

    Beams and effects

    The tractor cone and construction beam are shader effects in-engine (additive glow, scrolling rings). The transporter shimmer is a dissolve shader on the model plus a rising-sparkle particle column. Here they are drawn live in SVG so the timing can be judged.

    Gameplay hooks

    The building becomes selectable at touchdown (6.0 s) and finishes at 20.0 s. Unfurling is where the hub is most vulnerable: an attack before 11 s could cancel the build. The drop can be interrupted: shooting down the ship during the tractor phase loses the pod.
