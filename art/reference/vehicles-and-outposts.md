# Reference art: vehicles and outposts (owner, 2026-11-04)

The owner shared five reference images "for later". They are third-party art, and this repo is public, so the images
are not committed here; the owner has the originals. These notes capture what to take from each one when we model the
surface vehicles, the shuttle and the colony buildings.

## 1. Six-wheeled ground assault vehicle (desert)
- A long, boxy armoured carrier on six big wheels. The cab is wedge-fronted, with a huge green-tinted windscreen
  wrapping round the front corners.
- **Paint:** white with red flash stripes and a hull number ("24") on the side. Scuffed and battered paint, with
  desert dust kicked up behind.
- **Roof:** a light bar, a whip antenna and a hatch where a gunner stands behind a pintle-mounted phaser.
- **Use for:** a combat or escort ground unit, and for the general look of our surface vehicles (rover, crawler, survey team).

## 2. Starfleet "Pigeon class" lander and its "Grizzly Bear" G.A.V.
- **Lander:** an angular, wedge-shaped shuttle with grey panelled plating, blue and red accent panels and a hull
  number ("04").
  - A nose phaser, and a rear loading ramp that folds down.
  - A dropship for troops and ground vehicles (crew 2, carries 6; warp 5).
- **G.A.V. (ground assault vehicle):** six-wheel drive and steering, two phaser banks, high-powered spot lamps on
  the roof, and antigravs so it can hover. It deploys from the lander when transporters aren't available.
- **Use for:**
  - The orbit view's shuttle (Shuttle: Rover / Crawler / Team) as a real model that lands with its ramp down.
  - A G.A.V.-style unit that rolls out of it.
  - The Vehicle Bay's units.

## 3. Colony on a desert world (warm sunset)
- **Control tower:** a saucer cap with a windowed band on a tapered pylon, plus a second, squatter saucer-topped
  cone tower.
- **Terraced buildings:** long, low, stepped buildings with strips of windows.
- **Landing pad:** dark apron with yellow hazard stripes and circle markings. Shuttles are parked on it, each with a
  big number ("14"), and there's a boxy cargo hauler ("37") with a UFP emblem.
- **Storage and greenery:** rows of hexagonal cargo modules and arched storage sheds, a few trees, and small
  figures walking about.
- **Use for:**
  - Research lab or command (saucer tower), habitats (terraced blocks) and storage (hex modules, arched sheds).
  - A shuttle and landing pad next to the Colony Hub, beside the repair pad.
  - Hull numbers on our vehicles.

## 4. Spires and platforms (pink sky)
- Tall needle towers topped with stacked, pagoda-like rings.
- Twin saucer platforms on clusters of columns, with lift tubes.
- A big ribbed dome with a ring of windows and a tall antenna spire with a dish.
- Everything stands on a flat plinth.
- **Use for:** comms or sensor array (dome and spire), and the upper levels of the hub (Colonial Administration →
  Planetary Capital getting taller).

## 5. Low colony in a desert valley (matte-painting style)
- **Central dome:** a large dome with a glowing red beacon on top and sphere radomes around it.
- **Halls and pads:** long, flat halls with blue (solar?) roofs, a circular landing pad with a red centre and a ring
  of lights, and smaller round pads or pits.
- **Ground:** vehicle tracks worn into the dirt between buildings. The colony is hemmed in by steep, folded mountains.
- **Use for:** the Colony Hub (big dome, beacon light), reactors and solar halls, landing pads, and worn tracks
  around a busy base (render-only decals).

## Common threads
- **Colours:** white or light-grey hulls with one accent colour, plus hull numbers. This fits the player colour we
  already use.
- **Shapes:** domes, saucer caps and stepped blocks for buildings. Boxy six-wheelers and angular landers for vehicles.
- **Signs of life:** small details make a base feel lived in: beacons, spot lamps, hazard stripes, worn tracks and
  people walking.
